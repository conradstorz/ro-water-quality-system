Advertised: 8 relays <=10A 250VAC / 30VDC (resistive contact rating), isolated digital inputs, isolated RS485, W5500 Ethernet, RTC, TF card, 7–36V input, USB-C, header.
**Verify:** actual terminal/pinout, Modbus UART routing, RS485 termination/bias, relay GPIO and boot behavior, isolated input electrical thresholds, firmware flashing/recovery, actual input count, relay motor/inductive load derating, safety approvals.
Proposed relays: K1 motor-rated booster contactor coil control; K2 flush actuator on the valve/coil rail; K3 normally closed permeate dump valve on the tee drain branch, also on the valve/coil rail (energize to dump; unpowered = to tank, ADR-0006); K4 alarm; K5–K8 spare. Independent safety contacts wired in series with pump-enable circuit. No direct 120VAC motor switching via board relay.
Power: 24V supply (the same supply as the RJ45 node bus; `hardware/power/POWER_AND_WIRING.md`) to 7–36V board input. Valve and contactor-coil rail (K2, K3, K1 coil): 12 V in the earlier plan, final voltage and supply TBD. 5V regulated rail for sensor electronics as required; do not backfeed USB and external input without checking manual.
